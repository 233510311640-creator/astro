## Previous bundle: 10.1.0

Implemented: bounded explicit PDF documents for ask, answer, summarize and two-to-four-source
comparison. A PDF may be at most 8 MiB / 100 pages; extraction is capped at 64 KiB per page and
1 MiB total. Exact evidence includes PDF page and line references. Text and PDF sources may be mixed.

The separately pinned parser child receives only verified PDF bytes and returns a strictly validated
page/text structure. It receives no path, grant store, question, model connection or action engine.

return no text. Folder search remains plain-text only, model-free and without a saved index.

## Later work

Office parsing, OCR, PDFs larger than 8 MiB / 100 pages and persistent full-library indexing need
separate untrusted-parser, privacy and resource designs. They are not part of 10.1.0.
Broader app workflows, durable tasks, scheduling, reviewed memory, voice, research OS containment and
concurrency remain future work. Any runtime tuning must use actual measurements; none are inferred
from an installation success. Existing standard-user approvals, rollback, local-model roles, no paid
API, no hidden retries, automatic downloads/unloads, countdowns or artificial waiting remain.
